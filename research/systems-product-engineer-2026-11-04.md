## Arah positioning

**Systems & Product Engineer** menjadi identitas utama. Penjelasan yang menyertainya: aplikasi modular, layanan backend, integrasi sistem, dan infrastruktur untuk pengembang.

Yang menghubungkan bidang-bidang tersebut adalah ketertarikan pada cara sebuah produk bekerja: batas modul, alur data, komunikasi antarlayanan, penyimpanan lokal, worker, dan pengalaman pengembang. Judul ini perlu selalu dijelaskan dengan konteks perangkat lunak agar tidak tertukar dengan administrasi sistem atau infrastruktur jaringan.

Nada senior berasal dari ketepatan menjelaskan lingkup, keputusan, dan konsekuensinya. Tidak perlu menambahkan kata “Senior”, skala pengguna, atau jaminan keandalan yang belum dibuktikan. Modularitas dapat disebut sebagai pendekatan; reliability dan scalability perlu dijelaskan melalui keputusan dan bukti, bukan dijanjikan sebagai sifat semua proyek.

Fakta dasar: ORCA, Ocaty, dan Oxelot merupakan proyek pribadi yang dikerjakan dari perencanaan sampai pengembangan, deployment, branding, dan marketing. Pengalaman R&D Developer di PT Semesta Teknologi Informatika tetap merupakan pengalaman kerja yang terpisah. RabbitMQ Chat diposisikan sebagai eksplorasi komunikasi berbasis pesan.

## 8. Template studi kasus

```markdown
# [Project name] — [what it is]

[One sentence describing the product and its current stage.]

## Problem
Who encounters the problem? What are they trying to do?
Describe an observed need; distinguish it from an assumption.

## Context and constraints
Personal, employment, or client work? What was already present?
State relevant browser, connectivity, time, data, or deployment constraints.

## My role
What did I own? What did someone else own?
Separate implementation, product decisions, and business responsibilities.

## System design
Show the important components and one end-to-end data flow.
Include a diagram only if it makes a relationship clearer.

## Technical decisions
For each important choice: context → alternatives → decision → reason.
Link to implementation, an ADR, or another inspectable artifact.

## Trade-offs
What improved, and what became harder?
What limitation remains, and when would this decision change?

## Result or outcome
What exists now and what can someone inspect or try?
For a measured result: give the metric, baseline, environment, date, and method.
If there is no measurement, describe the delivered capability and its boundary.

## What I learned
What assumption changed because of the work?
What would I repeat or do differently? Use an actual reflection, not a slogan.
```

Untuk website, tampilkan ringkasan masalah, peran, dan hasil di bagian utama; detail panjang dapat berada di halaman khusus. Jangan memaksa delapan subbagian panjang masuk satu viewport. Untuk README, pertahankan instruksi menjalankan dan status proyek di luar studi kasus bila dibutuhkan oleh pengguna library.

### Ocaty — modular business application platform

**Problem.** A business product can span commerce, CRM, and ERP while still needing clear boundaries between its parts. Ocaty explores how those capabilities can be developed as modules with shared application infrastructure.

**Context and constraints.** This is a personal product project. Its scope includes separately developed frontend modules and backend services; individual modules can be at different stages of development.

**My role.** I handle product planning, branding, development, deployment, and marketing. My technical work includes frontend modules, shared UI components, and account and notification services.

**System design.** React modules are connected through Module Federation. FastAPI services and PostgreSQL handle the backend and data layer, with Docker used for deployment workflows.

**Technical decisions.** The architecture separates frontend modules from backend services while sharing selected application components. Module Federation is the integration mechanism for the frontend modules.

**Trade-offs.** Independent module boundaries create room for separate development, while shared contracts and dependencies still need coordination. Modularity does not remove integration work or prove scalability by itself.

**Result or outcome.** A modular business application platform under development, with inspectable frontend and service work. This case does not claim that every commerce, CRM, or ERP module is complete, or that the platform has a measured business impact.

**What I learned.** I learned to understand what someone wants to achieve before judging their product idea by the competitors I know or the implementation I would prefer. Development can reveal an opportunity that was not obvious to me at the start. That makes understanding the other person's perspective part of the engineering work, alongside testing the assumptions behind the idea.

**Editorial basis.** The final paragraph is based on NurHabib's reflection supplied during this task. It does not describe Ocaty as paid client work or imply that the opportunity has been commercially validated. A clearer understanding after development is a learning outcome, not evidence of market demand.

### Oxelot — offline-first developer infrastructure

**Problem.** Web applications that need local storage and background processing should not have to couple every product feature to low-level browser APIs. Synchronization adds a separate responsibility when connectivity changes.

**Context and constraints.** Oxelot is personal work on developer infrastructure for browser applications. Storage, worker execution, and synchronization operate within browser capabilities and lifecycle constraints.

**My role.** I handle the project from planning through implementation and deployment, including its branding and marketing.

**System design.** The project uses TypeScript, OPFS, SQLite through WebAssembly, and Web Workers. Its scope includes storage, worker management, and offline data synchronization.

**Technical decisions.** Browser-local storage and background execution are exposed through a library rather than positioned as a complete application framework. The public repository describes API contracts, architecture, implemented phases, and an ongoing roadmap.

**Trade-offs.** This approach exposes browser-specific capabilities while requiring explicit treatment of worker communication, lifecycle, storage behavior, and synchronization boundaries. Using OPFS or a worker does not establish durability or performance guarantees across devices.

**Result or outcome.** Source code and technical documentation are available for review. This summary does not repeat repository performance targets as independently measured results, or treat every roadmap item as released.

**What I learned.** Personal reflection still to confirm. Suggested question: which storage, worker, or synchronization assumption changed after implementation? Publish the specific answer with the code or experiment that changed it.

**Source:** [Oxelot repository and current status](https://github.com/NurHabibAssolihudin/Oxelot).

### RabbitMQ Chat — event-driven communication experiment

**Problem.** Explore how a message moves from a web application through a broker and reaches connected browsers, separating message production from realtime delivery.

**Context and constraints.** A communication experiment using FastAPI, RabbitMQ, and WebSockets. The project documentation describes separate user-facing applications and a receiver service.

**My role.** I built the experiment to explore realtime communication, queues, and event-driven data flow.

**System design.** A browser submits a message to a FastAPI application's HTTP endpoint. The application publishes to RabbitMQ. A receiver consumes the queue and broadcasts updates to connected WebSocket clients.

**Technical decisions.** RabbitMQ sits between the message-producing applications and the receiver. WebSockets provide the final live connection to the browser; they are not the broker.

**Trade-offs.** The broker introduces an extra process and operational responsibility compared with direct application-to-application communication. A queue alone does not establish chat history, authorization, reconnection recovery, or end-to-end delivery guarantees.

**Result or outcome.** A repository with a documented multi-process chat example and instructions for trying the message flow. This case is not a claim of production scale or a replacement for a complete messaging product.

**What I learned.** Personal reflection still to confirm. Suggested question: what became easier or harder when the receiver consumed a queue instead of receiving messages directly?

**Sources:** [RabbitMQ Chat README](https://github.com/NurHabibAssolihudin/RabbitMQ-Chat-App/blob/main/README.md), [RabbitMQ producer/queue/consumer model](https://www.rabbitmq.com/tutorials/tutorial-one-python). The project flow follows its README; broker documentation supplies terminology, not evidence that the project has particular delivery guarantees.
