Notes on making worlds.

The rules I keep coming back to when a world has to hold together.

  1. Moment before mechanics

    Build the feeling first, then the systems that hold it up. A mechanic without a feeling behind it is just a rule.

  2. Worlds are systems too

    Characters, rules and consequences: narrative runs on cause and effect. The best worlds are the ones that answer back.

  3. Story under the surface

    What you see is the tip of a world built underneath. The rest lives in notes, failures and quiet details nobody is meant to notice.

  4. Ship small worlds often

    Every project is a moon. The studio is the orbit they share, and it only grows by finishing things.
